A yellow paper boat drifts along a narrow rain-filled gutter beside a gray curb, gently bobbing as raindrops spread overlapping ripples and push tiny leaves past its folded hull. In one continuous move, the camera tracks slowly alongside at water level, then gently pulls back as the boat reaches a storm drain, revealing water streaming between the dark grate bars. Photorealistic macro cinematography, shallow depth of field, crisp wet paper texture, and cool overcast light reflected across the rippling water.
