A yellow paper boat drifts and gently bobs along a narrow rain-filled gutter beside a gray curb, while falling raindrops spread overlapping ripples across the water and nudge tiny leaves past its folded hull. In photorealistic macro cinematography under cool overcast light, the camera tracks slowly alongside the boat at water level, capturing wet paper texture and shimmering ripples with shallow depth of field, then gently pulls back as the boat reaches a storm drain, revealing the grate and converging runoff.
