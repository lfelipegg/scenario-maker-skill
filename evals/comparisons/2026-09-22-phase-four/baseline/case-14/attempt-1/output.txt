A yellow paper boat drifts and gently bobs along a narrow rain-filled gutter beside a gray concrete curb, while falling raindrops spread overlapping ripples and nudge tiny leaves past its folded hull. In one continuous shot, the camera tracks slowly alongside at water level, then gently pulls back as the boat reaches a storm drain, revealing water streaming between the dark metal bars. Photorealistic macro cinematography, shallow depth of field, crisp paper creases, wet concrete textures, and cool overcast light reflected on the moving water.
