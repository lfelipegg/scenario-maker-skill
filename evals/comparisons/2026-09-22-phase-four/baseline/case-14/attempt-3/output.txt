A yellow paper boat drifts and gently bobs along a narrow rain-filled gutter beside a gray concrete curb, while falling raindrops spread overlapping ripples and nudge tiny leaves past its folded hull. In one continuous shot, the camera tracks slowly alongside at water level, then gently pulls back as the boat reaches a storm drain, revealing water swirling toward the dark grate. Photorealistic macro cinematography, shallow depth of field, detailed wet paper and concrete textures, cool overcast light and soft reflections.
