A yellow paper boat drifts slowly along a narrow rain-filled gutter beside a wet gray curb, gently bobbing as raindrops spread overlapping ripples and nudge tiny leaves past its folded hull. In one continuous shot, the camera tracks alongside at water level, then gently pulls back as the boat reaches a storm drain, revealing water streaming between the dark metal bars.
