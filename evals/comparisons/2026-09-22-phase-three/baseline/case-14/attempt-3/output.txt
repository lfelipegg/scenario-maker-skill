A yellow paper boat drifts and gently bobs along a narrow rain-filled gutter beside a wet gray curb, while falling raindrops spread overlapping ripples and nudge tiny leaves past its folded hull. In one continuous shot, the camera tracks slowly alongside at water level, then gently pulls back as the boat reaches a storm drain, revealing water streaming between the dark metal bars. Photorealistic macro cinematography, shallow depth of field, crisp paper fibers and wet concrete textures, cool overcast light and soft reflections.
