A yellow paper boat drifts along a narrow rain-filled gutter beside a gray curb, its folded paper texture sharply visible as raindrops spread overlapping ripples across the water and push tiny leaves past it. In one continuous low, water-level tracking move, the camera slowly follows alongside the boat, then gently pulls back as it reaches a storm drain, widening the view of the gutter in photorealistic macro cinematography with shallow depth of field and cool, soft overcast light.
