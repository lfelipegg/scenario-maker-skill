A yellow paper boat drifts and gently bobs along a narrow rain-filled gutter beside a wet gray curb, while falling raindrops spread overlapping ripples and nudge tiny leaves past its folded hull. In one continuous move, the camera tracks slowly alongside at water level, then gently pulls back as the boat reaches a storm drain, revealing water streaming between the dark metal bars. Photorealistic macro cinematography, shallow depth of field, crisp paper texture, cool overcast light, and soft reflections on rain-darkened concrete.
