A yellow paper boat drifts slowly along a narrow rain-filled gutter beside a gray curb, gently bobbing as raindrops scatter overlapping ripples and nudge tiny leaves past its folded hull. In one continuous move, the camera tracks alongside at water level, then gently pulls back as the boat reaches a storm drain, revealing rainwater streaming toward the grate. Photorealistic macro cinematography, shallow depth of field, crisp paper creases and wet concrete textures, cool overcast light with soft reflections on the water.
