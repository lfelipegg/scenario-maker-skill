A yellow paper boat drifts along a narrow rain-filled gutter beside a gray curb, its folded paper texture crisp against the wet concrete. Raindrops strike the water, sending overlapping ripples outward and nudging tiny leaves past the boat. The camera tracks slowly alongside at water level, then gently pulls back as the boat reaches a storm drain. Photorealistic macro cinematography, shallow depth of field, cool overcast light, and soft reflections across the rippling water.
