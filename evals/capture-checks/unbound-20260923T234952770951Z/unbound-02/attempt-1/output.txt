A lone skater stops on a frozen lake at night, facing a pale light in the distance beneath the ice. The camera tracks low as the glow glides closer, resolving into an enormous luminous jellyfish. Its pulsing bell passes directly beneath the skater’s motionless boots, illuminating trapped bubbles and branching cracks. The camera tilts downward as trailing filaments fill the frame with blue light. Photorealistic, moonless darkness, one continuous shot.
