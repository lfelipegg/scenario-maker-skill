1. A freestanding limestone doorway rises from a mirror-flat salt desert, its opening filled by a vertical ocean. A diver in a copper helmet stands at the threshold, one gloved hand submerged beyond the frame; silver fish gather around the fingers. Wide, symmetrical composition, hard noon sunlight, turquoise water against chalk-white ground, surreal photographic realism.

2. A baker in a flour-dusted apron kneels in a brick bakery doorway, offering a tiny loaf to a fox on the snowy step. Warm oven light outlines the animal’s whiskers and spills across blue snow; cooling racks crowd the dim interior. Eye-level framing from the narrow street, intimate documentary photography, softly falling snow.

3. A monumental doorway cut into a black cliff frames an immense white moth, its folded wings filling the opening like patterned curtains. A scarlet-robed figure stands beneath its antennae on a narrow stone stairway above clouds. Low-angle composition, cold dawn rim light, stark scale contrast, finely textured charcoal-and-gouache illustration.
