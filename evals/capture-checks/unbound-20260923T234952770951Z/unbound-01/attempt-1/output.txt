A freestanding doorway rises from a vast salt flat beneath a violet dusk sky. Inside its weathered frame, a sunlit ocean stands vertically, a breaking wave suspended at the threshold. Seawater spills onto the cracked salt around a pair of abandoned boots. Low, wide framing emphasizes the doorway’s isolation; warm marine light catches peeling blue paint and glitters across the spreading puddle. Surreal fine-art photography with tactile, realistic textures.
