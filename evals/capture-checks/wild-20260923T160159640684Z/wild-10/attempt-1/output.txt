1. An abandoned greenhouse contains a miniature eclipse: a black sphere hangs above cracked planting beds, its white corona illuminating tangled vines. Rusted iron ribs and shattered roof panes frame the sphere; every sunflower bends toward it. Symmetrical aisle-level composition, silver light, deep green shadows, surreal botanical photography.

2. An abandoned greenhouse lies submerged beneath a turquoise sea, its broken glass walls enclosing towering coral shaped like orchids. Fish thread through rusted watering pipes, and pale anemones fill overturned terracotta pots. A whale’s silhouette eclipses the roof overhead. Wide underwater view, rippling sunlight, intricate textures of salt-eaten metal.

3. An abandoned greenhouse straddles a vast fissure, suspended by the colossal roots of a single flowering tree. Its buckled iron frame and shattered glazing remain recognizable above the abyss; empty planting benches tilt beneath exposed roots. Pink petals blanket the ruined central aisle. Dramatic aerial perspective, pale dawn mist, monumental botanical scale.
