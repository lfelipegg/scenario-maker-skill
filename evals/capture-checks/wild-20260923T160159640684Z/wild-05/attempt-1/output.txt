**A — Wild, clothing-only**  
A moss-green coat built from overlapping wool panels shaped like unfurling fern fronds. A sculptural spiral collar flows into an asymmetric fastening of curled bronze hooks; raised vein-like seams branch down the sleeves. The long, deeply split hem reveals dark green pleated lining, echoing tightly folded leaves. Garment only, without a wearer.

**B — Wild, isolated asset**  
An isolated brass compass with cracked glass, its case sculpted into concentric, offset rings like a miniature impact crater. A jagged fracture radiates from the needle’s pivot, echoed by engraved fissures across the brass bezel. Beneath the glass, a blackened compass rose surrounds a slender red-enamel needle; verdigris collects inside the recessed markings. No supporting objects or scenery.
