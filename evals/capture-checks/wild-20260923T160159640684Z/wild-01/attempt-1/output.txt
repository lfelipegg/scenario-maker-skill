A night market fills the exposed gears of a colossal clock tower, its stopped brass teeth forming terraces of crowded stalls. Vendors ladle luminous moon-shaped dumplings beneath parchment lanterns; shoppers cross narrow bridges between cogs. In the foreground, a cook lifts a bamboo steamer, silver vapor curling across warm copper machinery. Wide-angle view through the skeletal clock face, with an indigo city far below; richly textured cinematic fantasy.
