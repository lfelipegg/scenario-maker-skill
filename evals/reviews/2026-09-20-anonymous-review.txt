C09-QUALITY [advisory; due Phase 2]

Authority: https://github.com/lfelipegg/scenario-maker-skill/issues/18#issuecomment-5746540497

ACTUAL OUTPUT — BEGIN VERBATIM
**Overall:** The prompt establishes a mood, but gives little control over what will actually be visible. It works better as an open-ended creative seed than as a clear image specification.

### Strengths
- **Simple subject–setting structure:** A traveler in a location is easy to interpret, with no competing subjects or contradictory instructions.
- **Consistent tonal direction:** “Mysterious” and “cinematic” suggest an atmospheric image.
- **Creative flexibility:** Its brevity leaves room for unexpected results, if predictability is not the goal.

### Concrete problems
- **“Mysterious traveler” lacks visible cues.** Neither the traveler’s appearance nor their pose, clothing, expression, or activity is defined. Mystery is an intended impression, not a specified visual feature.
- **“Beautiful place” does not identify a setting.** It leaves landscape versus architecture, scale, weather, and environmental character unresolved. “Beautiful” expresses a preference rather than describing something drawable.
- **“Amazing lighting” is evaluative, not descriptive.** It establishes no light source, direction, softness, color, or contrast, so it offers little control over atmosphere or subject visibility.
- **“Dynamic composition” names an effect without defining its structure.** Framing, viewpoint, subject placement, depth, and directional movement are unspecified. It is unclear whether the traveler or the surrounding place should dominate.
- **“Cinematic” is broad.** It suggests a film-like treatment but does not settle the visual medium, palette, or camera treatment.

**Main issue:** Most of the wording describes desired impressions rather than observable features. The ambiguity is not inherently wrong, but it makes both visual clarity and compositional consistency difficult to predict.
ACTUAL OUTPUT — END VERBATIM
