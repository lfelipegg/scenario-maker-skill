A yellow paper boat drifts and gently bobs along a narrow rain-filled gutter beside a wet gray curb, its folded edges sharply detailed as raindrops scatter overlapping ripples and nudge tiny leaves past its hull. In one continuous shot, the camera tracks slowly alongside at water level, then gently pulls back as the boat reaches a storm drain, revealing water streaming through the dark grate. Photorealistic macro cinematography, shallow depth of field, cool overcast light, soft reflections on rain-darkened concrete.
