A yellow paper boat gently bobs along a narrow rain-filled gutter beside a gray curb, its folded edges crisp against the dark water as raindrops spread overlapping ripples and nudge tiny leaves past its hull. In one continuous movement, the camera tracks slowly alongside at water level, then gently pulls back as the boat reaches a storm drain, revealing the wet grate ahead. Photorealistic macro cinematography, shallow depth of field, detailed paper fibers and rain-darkened concrete, cool overcast light and soft reflections.
