A yellow paper boat drifts and gently bobs along a narrow rain-filled gutter beside a gray curb, as falling raindrops spread overlapping ripples and nudge tiny leaves past its folded hull. In one continuous movement, the camera tracks slowly alongside at water level, then gently pulls back as the boat reaches a storm drain, revealing the wet grate and converging current. Photorealistic macro cinematography, shallow depth of field, detailed paper fibers and glistening concrete under soft, cool overcast light.
